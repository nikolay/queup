How it works

Keep a tiny queue beside YouTube.

Connect YouTube once from the QueUp toolbar popup, then use the Que controls as you browse. QueUp keeps your saved videos in sync, opens the playlist when you are ready, and removes videos after you finish watching.

QueUp uses Google OAuth only to manage your own private Que playlist. No QueUp server stores your queue.
